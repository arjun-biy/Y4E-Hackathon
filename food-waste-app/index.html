Scale0000 g

Students drop their tray where they already do. A load cell under the plate weighs what's left, in grams.

A camera above the tray works out which foods came back. Broccoli? Half the milk? It never looks at faces or names.

The kitchen gets plain suggestions: a smaller side of broccoli, a share table for apples, milk as a choice.

Privacy

Tracks food.
Not students.

No faces, no names, no student IDs. The station only records the menu item, how much came back, and an optional anonymous button press.
